## User Journey Transformation

### 1. Landing (First 5 seconds)
**Goal:** Make user think "I NEED this"

**Elements:**
- Hero: Real backtest results comparison
- Live demo: See strategy performance instantly
- Social proof: Verified strategy results
- Clear transformation: "You today vs You with Quvanti"

### 2. Agent Creation (The "WOW" Moment)
**Goal:** Prove value IMMEDIATELY

**Flow:**
1. Describe strategy → Auto-backtest runs
2. Show STUNNING results:
   - Your strategy: +47%
   - SPY benchmark: +12%
   - Opportunities caught: 23
   - Losses avoided: $3,400
3. Celebrate: "This beats 87% of retail traders!"
4. Offer deployment

### 3. Dashboard (Daily Engagement)
**Goal:** Show ongoing value delivery

**Metrics:**
- Opportunities captured (not just trades)
- Money saved (avoided bad trades)
- Time saved (hours not watching charts)
- Edge maintained (% above benchmark)

**Gamification:**
- Achievement badges
- Streak tracking
- Leaderboard (optional)
- Performance milestones

### 4. Pro Upsell (ROI-Based)
**Goal:** Make Pro a no-brainer investment

**Calculator:**
- Input: Your typical trade size
- Output: "If Pro saves you 1 bad trade = $XXX saved"
- ROI: "Pro pays for itself in X days"
- Social proof: "Pro users see 3.2x better returns"
